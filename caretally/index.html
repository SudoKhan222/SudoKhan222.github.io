Caretally support

Pet meds, insulin and seizure log for iPhone

Need help or found a problem?

Send a support request. Requests are visible publicly, so please don't include personal or health details about yourself.

Is Caretally veterinary advice?

No. Caretally keeps records and reminders. It never suggests doses or alert levels. Always follow your vet's instructions, and contact your vet or an emergency clinic if you're worried.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account. See the privacy policy.

What's free and what's in Caretally Pro?

Logging doses, readings and seizures, reminders, vet alert levels, the seizure timer and CSV export are free. Caretally Pro lets you track more than one pet.

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Caretally, tap Caretally Pro on the pet list, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Caretally Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders aren't showing up

Check that notifications are on for Caretally in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.
