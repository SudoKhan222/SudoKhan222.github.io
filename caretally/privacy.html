Caretally privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Caretally is a care log for pets on daily treatment. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

Your data stays on your device

Caretally stores your pets, medications, readings and log entries on your iPhone. There is no Caretally account and no Caretally server, so we never receive, see or store your data.

Family sharing (optional)

You can choose to share a pet with family, a partner or a pet sitter. When you do, that pet's records and photos are kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, and only you and the people you invite can see them. If someone shares a pet with you, it's stored in their iCloud. Apple handles this storage under Apple's privacy policy; we can't access it. Doses logged on a shared pet show the first name from the person's Apple Account, so everyone knows who gave it. Pets you don't share stay only on your iPhone. You can stop sharing, or leave a pet someone shared with you, at any time from the pet's page.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Caretally has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it shares nothing with third parties.

Reminders

Medication reminders are scheduled on your device using iOS notifications. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Caretally Pro is sold through Apple. We don't see your name, email address or payment details; the App Store only tells the app whether Caretally Pro is active. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Exports

When you export or share a log (for example as a CSV file for your vet), you choose where it goes. Caretally doesn't send it anywhere on its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting a pet removes its records from your device. Deleting a pet you shared also removes it from iCloud and from everyone you shared it with. Deleting the app removes all Caretally data from your device; pets you shared stay in your iCloud until you delete them in the app or remove Caretally's data in iCloud settings.

Children

Caretally doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Caretally support page.

Caretally keeps records and reminders. It is not veterinary advice.
